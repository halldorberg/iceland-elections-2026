Five things ship together.

1. New poll: Gallup lokakönnun 15. maí 2026
   Final Gallup poll in Reykjavík city election, published the day
   before kjördag. D 31.3 · S 21.5 · A 11.3 · M 10.9 · C 9.8 ·
   J 4.8 · B 4.7 · F 2.3 · P 2.3 · G 0.6 · R 0.4 — sum 99.9 (rounding).
   D'Hondt over 23 seats: D 8 · S 5 · A 3 · M 3 · C 2 · J 1 · B 1 —
   matches the article's narrative. Inserted at the top of the
   reykjavik POLLS array; the existing Vísir Kosningaspá 14. maí
   shifts to #2.
   https://www.ruv.is/frettir/innlent/2026-05-15-sjalfstaedisflokkurinn-a-siglingu-i-lokakonnun-gallups-475158

2. Poll-source toggle (3 pills) above the coalition cards
   New segmented control to switch the seat-source for the coalition
   enumeration:
     - "Meðaltal" — average of the two newest polls (default)
     - "Gallup 15. maí"
     - "Vísir 14. maí"
   The "Meðaltal" entry computes a synthetic poll via simple
   arithmetic mean of each party's percentage across the two source
   polls, then re-runs D'Hondt against the 23 seats so totals stay
   consistent (averaging seat counts directly would round to 22 or 24).
   Toggling re-enumerates MWCs, re-scores, re-sorts, re-renders, and
   closes the modal (stale). Choice persists in localStorage.
   i18n keys coalitionPollSourceLabel/Average/Gallup/Visir + tooltips
   in IS / EN / PL.

3. Mobile: last coalition card no longer cut off
   The fixed-position mestlesid-pill and outreach-btn (the
   "Ertu frambjóðandi?" button) at the bottom-right of every page
   overlap the bottom ~60 px of the scrollable cards container on
   phone-class viewports. Added padding-bottom: 80px to the cards
   container inside the ≤768 px media block so the last card scrolls
   clear of both floats.

4. Share button: ReferenceError fix
   The "Deila" button's click handler was calling shareURL() (defined
   at module top level), but that function isn't reachable from the
   IIFE — same module-scope quirk that bit escapeHtml earlier.
   Inlined a local doShare() + copyToClipboard() inside the IIFE.
   Native share-sheet on mobile, clipboard API on desktop, execCommand
   fallback, URL-in-toast as the last resort.
