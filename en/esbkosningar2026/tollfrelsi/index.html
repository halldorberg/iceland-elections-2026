🏭Tariff-free access and full processing at home

Full membership would abolish EU tariffs on processed seafood products and make it possible to fully process the catch in Iceland instead of exporting it unprocessed; the added value and jobs would then remain in the country.

The YES side points out that EU tariffs on processed seafood make it unprofitable to fully process the catch in Iceland; the raw material is therefore exported unprocessed and the added value is created abroad. Full membership would abolish these tariffs and make it possible to process the catch at home.

Authors such as Finn Torfa Magnússon argue that tariff-free access to the internal market could bring processing jobs and value creation back to Icelandic fishing communities.

EEA vs. EU - How Many Jobs Are We Losing Because of Tariffs?

Iceland is outside the EU customs union, and fisheries and agriculture do not enjoy full free movement of goods; tariffs and uncertainty about future trade terms deter billion-krona investments in full processing in Iceland. We should ask how many valuable jobs in food technology, biotechnology, research, and product development will never be created in Iceland because of the current competitive position.
