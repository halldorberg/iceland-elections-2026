    Arctic Sea Tours is located in Dalvik in the North of Iceland, not far from Akureyri.     Why Dalvík Is One of the Best Whale Watching Destinations in Iceland

    Whale watching in Iceland reaches its peak in Dalvík, a gem in North Iceland where the whales of Iceland thrive. The sheltered Eyjafjörður fjord, known for its deep volcanic fissures and natural geothermal activity, creates rich feeding grounds for marine life. Here, the mixing of warm and cold Arctic currents fuels an explosion of zooplankton and krill, which attracts many species — especially the magnificent humpback whales of Iceland.

    Thanks to these ideal feeding conditions, Dalvík whale watching offers some of the highest sighting success rates in the world — over 98% year-round and more than 99% in summer! Whether you're an enthusiast seeking Arctic adventures or simply dreaming of watching whales up close, Dalvík is truly one of the best places for whale watching in Iceland.
